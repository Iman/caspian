## What you may do

Everything the AGPL allows, which is a great deal.

Run this software for any purpose, including commercially. Study it. Modify it.
Distribute it. Charge for it. Run it as a service for other people, provided you
meet section 13 and offer them the source of your version.

Refer to this project by name accurately and factually. Saying "a fork of
Caspian-BYOC", "based on Caspian-BYOC", or "compatible with Caspian-BYOC" is
nominative use and is fine, provided it is true and provided it does not suggest
this project produced or endorses your version.

## What you may not do

Distribute a modified version under the name `Caspian`, `Caspian-BYOC`, or any
name likely to be confused with them. Rename it first. Section 7(c) of the
licence already requires you to mark a modified version as changed; this goes
further and asks you to stop using the name at all.

Use the name, the mark, the logo or the authors' names to solicit donations,
sponsorship, grants or any other funding. See `FUNDING.md`.

Present your version in a way that suggests it is this project, is endorsed by
this project, or is affiliated with it.

## Why this exists

This is software for people who need to reach the open internet under
censorship. Users of tools like this cannot easily verify what they are running
or who they are giving money to, and that makes the name worth something to
somebody willing to misuse it. A fork raising funds as `Caspian` takes money
from those users on the strength of work it did not do, and any failure of that
fork lands on this project's reputation, which is the only thing standing
between a user and a decision to trust a binary.

Forking is welcome. Trading on the name is not.

## Other people's names

This document reserves one name. It does not claim anybody else's, and this
section exists so the asymmetry is written down rather than assumed.

Caspian is built on other people's work. `Xray`, `XTLS`, `REALITY` and
`libXray` are names of the XTLS project. `Clash` and `Clash.Meta`,
`Shadowsocks`, `Trojan`, `VMess`, `VLESS` and `Hysteria` are names of protocols
and projects maintained by others. None of them is licensed to this project by
anything in this repository, and none of them is claimed by it.

Where those names appear here they are used descriptively: to say truthfully
what this software is built on, and which configurations it accepts. That is
the only use made of them. Nothing in this repository should be read as a claim
that any of those projects endorses, sponsors, reviews, or is affiliated with
Caspian. They do not, and they have not been asked to.

The engine is a pinned upstream module, linked unmodified, and the share-link
parser is XTLS's own, vendored with its MIT notice kept beside the source.
`NOTICE` records both, with the licence of every module read from that module's
own licence file.

This is the same courtesy the additional term in `NOTICE` asks of others about
this project's name. If any of those projects would rather their name were used
differently here, the contact in `FUNDING.md` reaches the author, and the
wording will be changed.
