## There is one official channel

Funding for this project is only ever solicited through channels the author
publishes on the project's own website.

No fork, mirror, rebuild, repackaging or derivative of this software is
authorised to collect funds in the name of this project or of its authors. If
you are asked to donate to `Caspian`, `Caspian-BYOC`, or to their authors,
anywhere other than the official channel, the money does not reach them and the
request is not endorsed.

## Why this file exists

Censorship-circumvention tools are given away, and the people who need them
often cannot check who they are giving money to. That combination attracts
somebody who will take a published project, change nothing of substance, and
raise money on the strength of the original's name and reputation.

The licence cannot stop that on its own, and it is honest to say why. This
program links code under GNU GPL version 3, which forbids adding restrictions on
USE. A licence term saying "you may not seek funding without permission" is not
available here and would not bind anyone if it were written.

What is available is the name. Under section 7(d) of the licence, in `NOTICE`,
the authors' and licensors' names may not be used for publicity, and soliciting
funds is publicity. Under `TRADEMARK.md`, the project name and logo are not
licensed at all. So a fork is free to exist, free to be sold, and free to ask
for money, on its own name and its own reputation, and not on this one.

## If you are a fork, this is how to stay clear of it

Rename before you distribute. Say what you changed. Ask for money under your own
name. Say plainly that you are not affiliated with this project. All of that is
permitted, and none of it needs anybody's permission.

## If you are a user

Before donating to anything calling itself Caspian, check it against the
official site. A project that is not on that list is not this one, whatever the
page says.
