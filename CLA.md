## What you confirm

That the work is yours to give: you wrote it, or you have the right to submit
it, and it is not covered by an agreement with an employer or anyone else that
would contradict this.

That you have identified any third-party code in your contribution and the
licence it carries. A file taken from elsewhere must arrive with its licence
text and its origin, the way `third_party/libxray-share/` does.

That you are not knowingly contributing anything covered by a patent you or
your employer intend to assert against users of this project.

## Why this exists

Without it, every contributor holds copyright in their own patch and nobody can
change the project's licence, add a term, or act against misuse without tracking
down each of them for permission. One contributor who has moved on, or who
disagrees, can freeze the whole project.

That is not hypothetical for software of this kind. This is a
censorship-circumvention tool, its name is worth misusing, and the measures in
`TRADEMARK.md` and `NOTICE` only work if one person can enforce them. Divided
copyright makes them unenforceable in practice.

## What this does NOT do

It does not make your contribution proprietary. The project is AGPL and the
reasons are in `NOTICE`: it links GPL-3.0 code, so GPL-family terms are not a
preference here, they are a requirement.

It does not let the author take the project closed. The GPL-3.0 code in the
dependency graph prevents that regardless of what any contributor agrees to.

It does not ask you to give up your own copyright.

## How to accept

Say so in your first pull request:

    I have read CLA.md and I agree to it.

## A limit worth stating plainly

This document records the author's position. It is not legal advice and it was
not drafted by a lawyer. If this project ever grows to the point where the
agreement matters commercially, replace it with a reviewed one.
