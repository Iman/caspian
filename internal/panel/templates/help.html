{{- /* Why there are two controls. This card is ahead of the glossary card below it on purpose. That card names every control in one line each, which is the right shape for looking one up and the wrong shape for the question people actually arrive with: there are two things here that stop my internet, so why are there two, and which one do I press. The reason it is its own card rather than a longer line in the glossary is that the answer is not about either control on its own. It is about what one of them takes down that the other leaves standing, and about the lockout that follows: a person holding only a phone that is ON the hotspot cannot undo a switch-off from that phone, because the network they were reaching the panel over has gone with it. That is the whole reason the cut exists, and it was written down in internal/privsvc/cut.go and nowhere a user could read it. The two halves are a .pair, so they sit side by side on a screen wide enough and stack in document order on a phone. The hazard is an .assurance under both, because it is a property of the pair and not of either column. */ -}}
